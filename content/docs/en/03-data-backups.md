Because FlowDesk is **Local-First**, your data never leaves your computer. Everything is stored locally in an open `SQLite` database file.

### Automatic Backups
By default, FlowDesk automatically creates daily backups of your database to prevent data loss in case of a crash or accidental deletion. You can find these backup files directly inside your application data directory.

### Best Practices
We highly recommend occasionally copying these `.db` backup files to an external hard drive, USB flash drive, or your own private cloud storage (like Google Drive or Dropbox) for extra safety.
