# System Requirements

FlowDesk is built with Tauri and Rust, making it extremely lightweight and efficient compared to standard Electron-based applications.

## Minimum Requirements

- **OS:** Windows 10/11, macOS 10.15+, or modern Linux distributions (Ubuntu, Fedora, Arch).
- **Processor:** Dual-core 1.5 GHz or faster.
- **RAM:** Minimum of 2 GB.
- **Storage:** Minimum of 150 MB free space for installation.

FlowDesk is highly optimized and barely impacts your laptop's battery when running in the background.
