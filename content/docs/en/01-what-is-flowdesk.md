# What is FlowDesk?

FlowDesk is the latest innovation in the world of productivity. Instead of storing your data on third-party cloud servers, FlowDesk stores your Projects, Tasks, and Notes directly on your local storage device.

## Why Choose FlowDesk?

1. **Absolute Privacy:** No tracking, no silent syncing, no hidden servers.
2. **Instant Performance:** Because everything runs locally, opening tasks and searching notes happens in milliseconds.
3. **Infinite Lifespan:** FlowDesk doesn't rely on monthly subscriptions. Once downloaded, the software is fully yours forever.
