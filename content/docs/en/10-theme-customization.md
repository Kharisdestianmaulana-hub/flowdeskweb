# Theme Customization

We understand that aesthetics matter for your working comfort. Open **Settings > Appearance** to adjust the app's visual style.

## Basic Theme Options
- **Light Mode:** Bright and clean theme.
- **Dark Mode:** Elegant dark theme, perfect for dim environments to reduce eye strain.
- **System:** Automatically follows your computer's system theme settings.

## Accent Colors
You can also change the color of buttons and text highlights. We provide 6 accent color options, ranging from *FlowDesk Blue*, *Emerald Green*, to *Sunset Orange*.
