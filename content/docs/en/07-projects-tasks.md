# Projects & Tasks

FlowDesk offers an intuitive Kanban system for managing your workflows.

## Creating a New Project
1. Open the **Projects** tab.
2. Click the **+ New Project** button.
3. Provide a name and description, then hit Enter.

## Using the Kanban Board
Inside each project, you can create custom columns/lists (e.g., *To Do*, *In Progress*, *Done*).
- **Add Task:** Click the `+` sign at the bottom of a column.
- **Move Task:** Use drag and drop to move tasks between columns.
- **Labels & Deadlines:** Click on a task to open the detail panel, where you can add color labels and due dates.
