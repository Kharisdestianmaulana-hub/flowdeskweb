FlowDesk v1.5.0 introduces **Local Workspace** – a magical way to collaborate without the cloud.

### How to Host a Session
To host a session:
1. Enter your private workspace.
2. Go to **Settings**.
3. Toggle **Local Workspace** on.

FlowDesk will use UDP broadcasting to advertise your session on your local network (Wi-Fi or LAN).

### How to Join a Session
For teammates to join, they just need to be connected to the same router or Wi-Fi network.
1. Select **Join** on their app home screen.
2. Select your broadcasted workspace.
3. Enter their name.
4. Wait for your approval.

All syncs happen in real-time via SignalR directly between your computers without hitting any external servers.
