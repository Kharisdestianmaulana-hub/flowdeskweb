Welcome to FlowDesk! 

Installation is straightforward. Simply download the `.exe` (for Windows) or `.dmg` (for macOS) from our [Download page](/en/download). 

Once installed, launch the application. You **do not need an account or internet connection** to start creating projects, tasks, and documents. Everything you do is instantly saved locally on your device.
