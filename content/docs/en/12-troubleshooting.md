# Troubleshooting

Here are a few initial steps you can take if FlowDesk is acting unresponsive:

## Step One: Reload
Sometimes the UI just needs a quick refresh.
Press `Ctrl/Cmd + R` to restart the User Interface without closing the app.

## Step Two: Clear Cache
1. Go to **Settings > Advanced**.
2. Click the **Clear Local Cache** button.
3. The app will close and reopen automatically. Don't worry, your core data files will not be deleted.

If problems persist, you can check the application logs in your installation folder.
