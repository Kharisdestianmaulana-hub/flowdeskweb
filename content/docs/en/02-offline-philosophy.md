# Offline-First Philosophy

In the modern era, almost all applications force you to connect to the internet. We at FlowDesk challenge this trend with an **Offline-First** approach.

## Our Core Principles

- **Data Sovereignty:** Your data is yours. We have no right to view, process, or sell it.
- **Format Transparency:** All your data is saved in standard `.json` and `.md` formats. You can open them with other apps anytime.
- **Long-term Resilience:** Because you don't rely on our servers, FlowDesk will continue to work even if we disappear 10 years from now.

Join the local-first revolution!
